Bee Finder

Designed to assist with the New York Times Spelling Bee Puzzle

Sometimes the need for instant gratification is too strong, and one simply cannot wait a day to see what those words are that are standing between them and a Genius-level badge for the day. I have created a tool for just that occasion. I prefer to use it when I am stuck and advocate only responsible, admirable use, but if taunting and tormenting fellow players is your thing, have at it.

How to Use

Enter the letters from the puzzle in the inputs below, bee amazed. Please note that this utility will yield more words than the puzzle, as I am using data from a broader source (details are on my github page). I view it as a great opportunity to learn some new, somewhat outrageous words. Enjoy!
